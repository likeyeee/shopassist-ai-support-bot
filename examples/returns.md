# Returns & exchanges

You can return unworn, unwashed items with their original tags within 30 days of delivery. Start a return by contacting support with your order number. Final-sale items and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the return. Original shipping fees are non-refundable. US customers receive a prepaid return label. International customers cover return shipping.

## Exchanges

Size exchanges are free for US orders, subject to stock availability. Contact support with your order number and preferred size. Exceptions, refund approvals, and individual return processing require a human agent.
