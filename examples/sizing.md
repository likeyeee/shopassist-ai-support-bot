# Size guide

Our everyday collection uses US unisex sizing and has a relaxed fit. If you are between sizes, choose the smaller size for a closer fit or the larger size for a looser fit.

## Measurements

Measure around the fullest part of your chest. Size S: 34–36 inches. Size M: 38–40 inches. Size L: 42–44 inches. Size XL: 46–48 inches. Measurements refer to body measurements, not garment dimensions.

## Care

Wash cold and air dry to maintain the fit. Do not bleach. Check the garment care label for fabric-specific instructions.
