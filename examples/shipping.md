# Shipping & delivery

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Standard shipping to the United States takes 5–8 business days after dispatch. Express shipping takes 2–3 business days. Shipping is free on orders over $75; otherwise standard shipping costs $6.95.

## International shipping

We ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the European Union. International delivery takes 7–14 business days. Customs duties are paid by the recipient.

## Tracking

Tracking is emailed when your order ships. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support with your order number. Only a human agent with order-system access can look up an individual order or change an address.
